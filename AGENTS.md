# N2Getaways repository instructions

For “Draft the next approved N2Getaways topic” or other editorial work, follow `editorial/README.md`. Use the existing chat for research/writing and the repository's editorial commands. Do not use paid inference/research APIs, add subscriptions, or activate scheduled workflows.

Only queue topics marked approved may be drafted. Always inspect existing coverage and remote topic branch/PR reservations. Keep generated articles `draft: true` and open draft PRs. Topic approval and permission to draft never authorize publication. Do not set approval fields, mark an article ready to publish, merge its PR, or enable auto-merge without explicit owner approval of that specific article. Preserve the production domain, email DNS and existing WordPress source. Include factual research sources, claim-verification flags, existing internal links and approved image keys; never fabricate personal experiences or image URLs.
