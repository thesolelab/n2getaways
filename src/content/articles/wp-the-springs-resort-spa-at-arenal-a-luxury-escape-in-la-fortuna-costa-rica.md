<p>If you&#x27;re looking for one of the most spectacular luxury resorts in Costa Rica, <strong>The Springs Resort &amp; Spa at Arenal</strong> should be at the top of your list.</p>

<p>Located in <strong>La Fortuna de San Carlos in the northern highlands of Costa Rica</strong>, this award-winning five-star resort offers breathtaking views of <strong>Arenal Volcano and the Arenal River</strong> while surrounding guests with rainforest, wildlife, and natural hot springs.</p>

<p>The property blends adventure, relaxation, and luxury into one unforgettable experience, making it one of the most popular destinations for travelers exploring Costa Rica’s Arenal region.</p>

## Where Is The Springs Resort & Spa at Arenal Located?

<p>The resort sits just outside <strong>La Fortuna</strong>, one of Costa Rica’s most famous adventure destinations.</p>

<p>Surrounded by lush rainforest and volcanic landscapes, the property overlooks:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Arenal Volcano</strong></li>

<li><strong>The Arenal River Valley</strong></li>

<li><strong>Protected rainforest</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>This location allows visitors to experience both <strong>luxury resort living and outdoor adventure</strong> in one place.</p>

<hr>

## Getting to The Springs Resort & Spa at Arenal

<p>One important thing to know before visiting is that <strong>getting to La Fortuna requires a drive</strong>.</p>

<p>From Costa Rica’s major international airports:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>San José International Airport (SJO)</strong> – about <strong>2.5 to 3 hours</strong></li>

<li><strong>Liberia International Airport (LIR)</strong> – about <strong>3 to 3.5 hours</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>While rental cars are available, many visitors prefer taking a <strong>private taxi or shuttle</strong>.</p>

<p>Some of the roads heading toward the Arenal region include <strong>rural mountain routes and off-road sections</strong>, so if you&#x27;re unfamiliar with the area, a <strong>professional driver is highly recommended</strong>.</p>

<p>The drive itself is scenic, passing through Costa Rican countryside, jungle landscapes, and small mountain towns.</p>

## Resort Activities and Excursions

<p>While many resort amenities are included with your stay, several excursions are <strong>available for an additional cost</strong>.</p>

<p>These guided experiences are some of the most popular activities guests book during their visit.</p>

### Horseback Riding Around Arenal

<p>Horseback riding is one of the best ways to experience the rainforest landscapes surrounding the resort.</p>

<p>Trails wind through jungle terrain and scenic viewpoints where riders can see <strong>Arenal Volcano and the surrounding valley</strong>.</p>

<p>It&#x27;s also a great opportunity to spot wildlife like monkeys and tropical birds.</p>

<hr>

### ATV Adventures Through the Jungle

<p>For travelers looking for something more adventurous, <strong>ATV tours</strong> are a popular option.</p>

<p>These excursions take guests along rugged jungle paths and scenic viewpoints that showcase Costa Rica’s diverse terrain.</p>

<p>Expect muddy trails, river crossings, waterfalls, and incredible rainforest scenery. </p>

<hr>

### Kayaking the Arenal Region

<p>Kayaking excursions allow visitors to explore Costa Rica’s waterways while surrounded by jungle landscapes.</p>

<p>Paddling through calm waters near the Arenal region offers a unique perspective of the rainforest and volcano views.</p>

<hr>

## Popular Experiences at the Resort

<p>Beyond traditional excursions, the resort also offers unique experiences that highlight Costa Rica’s wildlife and natural environment.</p>

### Wildlife Sanctuary Tour

<p>Costa Rica is known for its incredible biodiversity, and guests have opportunities to see wildlife through a guided tour.</p>

<p>Seeing these animals in their natural habitat is often one of the most memorable parts of a trip to Arenal.</p>

<hr>

### Bird Watching

<p>This tour starts early! You&#x27;ll depart from the lobby at 6am and will spend about 2 hours going through different environments viewing as many as 40 different bird species.</p>

<p>Binoculars are a must-have for this adventure as well as long pants and closed-toed shoes. Pro tip: bring rain gear and mosquito repellant.</p>

<hr>

### Rock Wall Climbing & Rapelling

<p>The Club Rio Adventure Center has a 50-foot high rock climbing wall. The wall offers multiple challenge options for different skill levels.</p>

<p>No experience is needed for this adventure and climbing shoes are provided.</p>

<hr>

### Wildcat Feeding

<p>This hour long unique experience is truly one-of-a-kind. Watch experts feed the tropical wild felines in unique ways while learning about their natural behaviors.</p>

<p>This tour isn&#x27;t just limited to spotting wildcats in their natural environment. You&#x27;ll see toucans, sloths, caimans, and more!</p>

### How far is The Springs Resort & Spa at Arenal from the airport?

<p>The Springs Resort &amp; Spa at Arenal is approximately <strong>2.5 to 3 hours from San José International Airport (SJO)</strong> and <strong>3 to 3.5 hours from Liberia International Airport (LIR)</strong>.
