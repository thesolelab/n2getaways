## A Breakfast Option That's Worth the Wait

<p>If you&#x27;re spending time in Waikiki, there’s a good chance you’ve heard of <strong>Eggs &#x27;n Things</strong>.</p>

<p>Known for its iconic pancakes, long lines, and classic Hawaiian breakfast plates, this spot has become a must-visit for travelers looking to start their day near the beach.</p>

<p>Whether you’re heading out for a full day of exploring or just <a href="/lunch-at-lulus-waikiki-the-perfect-way-to-start-your-hawaii-vacation/">easing into vacation mode</a>, Eggs ’n Things is one of the most popular breakfast spots in Waikiki.</p>

<hr>

## A Classic Waikiki Breakfast Spot

<p>Located along Waikiki Beach, Eggs ’n Things offers a casual dining experience where you can enjoy breakfast with a view of the ocean just steps away.</p>

<p>It’s the kind of place where you’ll see:</p>

<ul>
<li>Visitors fresh off the beach</li>

<li>Early risers starting their day</li>

<li>Lines forming before peak hours</li>
</ul>

<p>Being able to sit down, eat, and take in the beach atmosphere right from your table makes it an easy first stop to kick off your day in Honolulu.</p>

<hr>

## What to Order at Eggs ’n Things

<p>This is where the spot really stands out.</p>

<p>Eggs ’n Things is best known for its <strong>fluffy pancakes topped with whipped cream and fresh fruit</strong>, and it definitely lives up to the hype.</p>

<p>From our visit, we enjoyed a mix of classic and local dishes that give you a true taste of Hawaii.</p>

<p>Popular menu items include:</p>

<ul>
<li>Pancakes with strawberries and whipped cream</li>

<li><strong>Hawaiian Loco Moco</strong></li>

<li><strong>Fresh Line Caught Ahi &amp; Eggs (served with rice for a more authentic experience)</strong></li>

<li><strong>Organic Açaí Fresh Fruit Waffle (dine-in only)</strong></li>

<li>Portuguese sausage, eggs, and rice</li>
</ul>

<p>Each table also comes with a selection of <strong>tropical syrups</strong>, including coconut, guava, and passion fruit, which adds a unique Hawaii touch to every meal.</p>

<hr>

## Be Prepared for a Wait

<p>One thing to know before you go — this place gets busy.</p>

<p>To beat the crowds, you’ll want to <strong>arrive before 9:00 AM</strong>.</p>

<p>When you get there, you’ll check in using the <strong>tablet at the entrance</strong> to join the queue, which makes the process simple even during busy times.</p>

<p>Lines can build quickly, especially during:</p>

<ul>
<li>Morning hours</li>

<li>Weekends</li>

<li>Peak travel seasons</li>
</ul>

<p>Getting there early makes a big difference and helps you start your day without a long wait.</p>

<hr>

## Location and Convenience

<p>Eggs ’n Things is located right in Waikiki, making it:</p>

<ul>
<li>Easy to walk to from most hotels</li>

<li>Right along the beach</li>

<li>A convenient stop before or after morning activities</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s a great option if you want something filling without having to travel far.</p>

<hr>

## Why It’s Worth Visiting

<p>While there are plenty of breakfast options in Waikiki, Eggs ’n Things stands out for its combination of:</p>

<ul>
<li>Classic breakfast comfort food</li>

<li>Local Hawaiian dishes</li>

<li>Beachfront location</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s not about being the most hidden spot — it’s about delivering a <strong>reliable, satisfying breakfast experience with a great view</strong>.</p>

<hr>

## Final Thoughts

<p>If you&#x27;re looking for a well-known, easy, and satisfying breakfast near Waikiki Beach, <strong>Eggs ’n Things</strong> is a solid choice.</p>

<p>Between the ocean views, generous portions, and variety of menu options, it’s an ideal way to start your day before heading out to explore Honolulu.</p>
