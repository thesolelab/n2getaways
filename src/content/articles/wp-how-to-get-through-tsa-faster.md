<p>Traveling right now isn’t business as usual.</p>

<p>With the ongoing government shutdown creating staffing shortages, TSA wait times have become unpredictable—and in some cases, extreme. Reports of lines stretching for hours at major airports like Atlanta (ATL) and Houston (IAH) are making it clear:</p>

<p>👉 Showing up and hoping for the best isn’t the move anymore.</p>

<p>If you’ve got a flight coming up, here’s how to stay ahead of the chaos and get through TSA faster right now.</p>

<p>Check out our <a href="/airports-without-tsa/">breakdown of airports without TSA</a> and what it really means for travelers.</p>

<hr>

## Why TSA Lines Are So Unpredictable Right Now

<p>TSA is still operating, but the shutdown is putting pressure on staffing.</p>

<p>That means:</p>

<ul>
<li>Fewer agents at certain checkpoints</li>

<li>Slower processing during peak hours</li>

<li>Longer and less predictable wait times</li>
</ul>

<p>At some major hubs, travelers are reporting <strong>multi-hour delays</strong>, especially during busy travel windows.</p>

<p>👉 If you’re wondering why this is happening, check out our breakdown of airports without TSA and what it actually means.</p>

<hr>

## 1. Check TSA Wait Times Before You Leave

<p>Right now, you can’t rely on your “normal” airport timing.</p>

<p>Some days move smoothly. Others don’t.</p>

### What to use:

<ul>
<li><strong>MyTSA App</strong> → best overall for real-time estimates</li>

<li><strong>Airport websites</strong> → especially for major hubs like Houston or Atlanta</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 Pro tip: If wait times are already high before you leave, adjust your arrival time immediately.</p>

<hr>

## 2. Arrive Earlier Than You Think You Need To

<p>This isn’t the time to cut it close.</p>

### Current recommendation:

<ul>
<li><strong>2+ hours for domestic flights (minimum)</strong></li>

<li><strong>3–4 hours at major airports or peak times</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>It might feel excessive—but missing your flight feels worse.</p>

<hr>

## 3. Use TSA PreCheck, CLEAR, or Global Entry

<p>If you travel even a few times a year, these are worth it—especially right now.</p>

### TSA PreCheck

<ul>
<li>Keep shoes on</li>

<li>Leave electronics in your bag</li>

<li>Typically shorter, faster lines</li>
</ul>

### CLEAR

<ul>
<li>Uses biometrics to skip the ID line</li>

<li>Moves you directly to screening</li>
</ul>

### Global Entry

<ul>
<li>Best for international travelers</li>

<li>Includes TSA PreCheck</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 Right now, these programs aren’t just convenient—they can be the difference between making your flight or not.</p>

<hr>

## 4. Take Advantage of Digital ID (If Available)

<p>Some airports are now using <strong>touchless ID verification</strong> with facial recognition.</p>

<p>This allows you to:</p>

<ul>
<li>Skip manual ID checks</li>

<li>Move through faster with less interaction</li>
</ul>

<p>Availability is expanding, but it’s already active at many major airports with airlines like Delta, United, and American.</p>

<hr>

## 5. Pack Your Carry-On Like You Know What You’re Doing

<p>This is one of the biggest time-wasters at TSA.</p>

<p>Even with newer scanners, rules aren’t always consistent across lanes.</p>

### Stick to the basics:

<ul>
<li>Follow the <strong>3-1-1 liquids rule</strong> (3.4 oz, quart bag)</li>

<li>Keep electronics easy to remove</li>

<li>Avoid stacking devices on top of each other</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 “Dense” bags are getting flagged more often right now, which leads to manual checks.</p>

<hr>

## 6. Wear “Checkpoint-Ready” Clothing

<p>What you wear can either speed things up—or slow you down.</p>

### Go with:

<ul>
<li>Slip-on shoes</li>

<li>Minimal jewelry</li>

<li>Simple layers</li>
</ul>

### Avoid:

<ul>
<li>Heavy accessories</li>

<li>Boots with laces</li>

<li>Anything that takes time to remove</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 The goal is to move through security in one pass.</p>

<hr>

## 7. Be Fully Ready Before You Reach the Belt

<p>This is where a lot of people slow the line down.</p>

<p>Before you get to the front:</p>

<ul>
<li>Have your ID ready</li>

<li>Empty your pockets</li>

<li>Know what needs to come out of your bag</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 The less you hesitate, the faster everything moves.</p>

<hr>

## What’s Slowing TSA Lines Down the Most Right Now

<p>A few common mistakes are causing major delays:</p>

<ul>
<li>Liquids left in bags</li>

<li>Overpacked carry-ons</li>

<li>Electronics buried or stacked</li>

<li>Travelers reorganizing at the belt</li>
</ul>

<p>It might seem small, but these add up—especially when staffing is already limited.</p>

<hr>

## The Bottom Line

<p>TSA isn’t shutting down—but it is under pressure.</p>

<p>That means longer lines, slower processing, and less predictability at the airport.</p>

<p>The good news?</p>

<p>You don’t need a complicated strategy—just a smarter one.</p>

<ul>
<li>Arrive earlier</li>

<li>Pack intentionally</li>

<li>Stay ready</li>
</ul>

<p>Do that, and you’ll move through security a lot smoother than most travelers right now.</p>

## Final Thought

<p>Travel days feel a little more chaotic than usual—but they’re still manageable if you stay ahead of it.</p>
