<p>If you&#x27;re staying at <strong>The Springs Resort &amp; Spa at Arenal</strong>, you’re not just booking a luxury resort — you’re stepping into one of the most experience-packed destinations in Costa Rica.</p>

<p>Located in <strong>La Fortuna</strong>, with views of <strong>Arenal Volcano and the Arenal River</strong>, the resort offers a mix of relaxation, adventure, and wildlife experiences all in one place.</p>

<p>Whether you&#x27;re planning a full week or a quick getaway, here are the <strong>best things to do at The Springs Resort &amp; Spa at Arenal</strong> to make the most of your stay.</p>

<p>For a full breakdown of the resort, accommodations, and a complete travel plan, check out our <strong><a href="/the-springs-resort-spa-at-arenal-a-luxury-escape-in-la-fortuna-costa-rica/">Springs Resort &amp; Spa at Arenal Travel Guide and 7-Day Itinerary</a></strong>.</p>

<hr>

## Relax in the Natural Hot Springs

<p>One of the biggest highlights of the resort is its <strong>natural hot springs pools</strong>.</p>

<p>These mineral-rich pools are spread across the property, offering multiple temperature levels and incredible views of the rainforest and Arenal Volcano.</p>

<p>It’s the perfect way to:</p>

<ul>
<li>Start your morning</li>

<li>Recover after excursions</li>

<li>Wind down at sunset</li>
</ul>

<p>This alone is one of the main reasons travelers choose The Springs Resort.</p>

<hr>

## Go Horseback Riding with Volcano Views

<p>Horseback riding is one of the most popular activities at the resort — and for good reason.</p>

<p>The trails take you through:</p>

<ul>
<li>Rainforest paths</li>

<li>Open scenic viewpoints</li>

<li>Areas overlooking Arenal Volcano</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s a relaxed but immersive way to experience the landscape while also giving you opportunities to spot wildlife along the way.</p>

<p>If you captured photos during your ride, this is a great section to feature them.</p>

<hr>

## Take an ATV Jungle Adventure

<p>For something more high-energy, <strong>ATV tours</strong> are a must.</p>

<p>These guided excursions take you through rugged jungle terrain with:</p>

<ul>
<li>Muddy trails</li>

<li>River crossings</li>

<li>Elevated viewpoints</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s one of the best ways to explore areas that aren’t accessible on foot.</p>

<p>This activity is typically an <strong>additional cost</strong>, but it’s one of the most memorable experiences available at the resort.</p>

<hr>

## Kayak Through the Arenal Region

<p>If you’re looking for something more laid-back but still adventurous, kayaking offers a great balance.</p>

<p>Paddling through the Arenal region gives you:</p>

<ul>
<li>Peaceful water views</li>

<li>Rainforest surroundings</li>

<li>A different perspective of the landscape</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s a great option for a slower-paced day while still getting outside.</p>

<hr>

## Experience Wildlife

<p>Costa Rica is known for its biodiversity, and The Springs Resort gives you access to incredible wildlife experiences.</p>

<p>Guests often have opportunities to see:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Sloths in their natural habitat</strong></li>

<li>Go on a guided tour in the<strong> Wildlife Sanctuary </strong></li>

<li>Schedule a bird watching tour</li>
</ul>

<p>These guided experiences are perfect if you want to experience the rainforest beyond just the views.</p>

## Enjoy the Views of Arenal Volcano

<p>Sometimes the best activity is simply taking in the view.</p>

<p>The resort offers <strong>some of the best views of Arenal Volcano in Costa Rica</strong>, and depending on the weather, you can catch:</p>

<ul>
<li>Clear morning views</li>

<li>Cloud-covered dramatic scenes</li>

<li>Sunset lighting over the volcano</li>
</ul>

<p>This is where your <strong>hero image</strong> of the volcano should go.</p>

## How to Plan Your Time at The Springs Resort

<p>The key to enjoying The Springs Resort is balancing <strong>adventure and relaxation</strong>.</p>

<p>A simple way to structure your days:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Morning:</strong> Excursion (ATV, horseback riding, kayaking)</li>

<li><strong>Afternoon:</strong> Pool or hot springs</li>

<li><strong>Evening:</strong> Dinner + sunset views</li>
</ul>

<p>This approach keeps your trip from feeling rushed while still allowing you to experience everything the resort has to offer.</p>

## FAQ: Things to Do at The Springs Resort & Spa at Arenal

### What activities cost extra at The Springs Resort?

<p>Activities like horseback riding, ATV tours, and kayaking, are typically available at an additional cost. Many experiences can be booked directly through the resort.</p>

<hr>

### Can you see wildlife at The Springs Resort?

<p>Yes. The wildlife sanctuary is home to various animals.</p>

<hr>

### How many days should you stay at The Springs Resort?

<p>Most visitors stay <strong>3 to 5 days</strong>, which allows enough time to enjoy both the hot springs and several excursions without feeling rushed.</p>

<hr>

### Is The Springs Resort good for adventure travelers?

<p>Yes. The resort offers a mix of relaxation and adventure, making it ideal for travelers who want activities like ATV tours, horseback riding, and kayaking alongside luxury amenities.</p>
