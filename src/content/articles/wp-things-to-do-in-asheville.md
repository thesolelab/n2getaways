<p>Asheville is one of those cities that balances nature, food, and creativity without feeling overwhelming.</p>

<p>Between the Blue Ridge Mountains, a walkable downtown, and a strong local scene, it’s easy to fill a weekend without overplanning.</p>

<p>If you’re visiting for the first time, these are the <strong>best things to do in Asheville</strong>, from must-see attractions to local spots that give you a feel for the city.</p>

<hr>

## Visit the Biltmore Estate

<p>No trip to Asheville is complete without visiting the <strong>Biltmore Estate</strong>.</p>

<p>As the largest privately owned home in the U.S., it’s one of the most popular attractions in North Carolina.</p>

<p><strong>What to focus on:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>The Biltmore House tour</li>

<li>The Conservatory</li>

<li>The Lagoon for scenic views</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 Plan a morning visit to avoid crowds.</p>

<hr>

## Drive the Blue Ridge Parkway

<p>The <strong>Blue Ridge Parkway</strong> is one of the most scenic drives in the country.</p>

<p>Just minutes from downtown Asheville, it offers:</p>

<ul>
<li>Mountain overlooks</li>

<li>Easy hiking access</li>

<li>Sunset views</li>
</ul>

<p>One of the best stops is <strong>Craggy Pinnacle Trail</strong>, a short hike with a huge payoff at the top.</p>

<hr>

## Explore Downtown Asheville

<p>Downtown Asheville is compact, walkable, and full of character.</p>

<p>You can easily spend a few hours:</p>

<ul>
<li>Walking the <strong>Urban Trail</strong></li>

<li>Exploring local shops</li>

<li>Stopping at cafes and restaurants</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 It’s one of the easiest cities to explore without needing a plan.</p>

<hr>

## Visit the River Arts District

<p>The <strong><a href="https://www.riverartsdistrict.com/">River Arts District (RAD)</a></strong> is one of Asheville’s most unique areas, with over 200 artists working out of converted warehouses along the French Broad River.</p>

<p><strong>Where to start:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Depot Street area</li>

<li>Clingman Avenue studios</li>
</ul>

<p>From there, you can walk between buildings, check out working studios, and get a feel for Asheville’s creative scene.</p>

<hr>

## Experience Asheville’s Food Scene

<p>Asheville’s food scene goes deeper than just a few well-known spots — there’s a strong mix of casual, local favorites and elevated dining.</p>

<p>Here are a few great options that are consistently recommended:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Sunny Point Cafe</strong> – One of the most popular breakfast spots in the city (expect a wait, but worth it)</li>

<li><strong>White Duck Taco Shop</strong> – Creative tacos with unique flavors, especially convenient if you&#x27;re near the River Arts District</li>

<li><strong>Rhubarb</strong> – Farm-to-table spot in downtown with a more refined but still relaxed feel</li>

<li><strong>Chestnut</strong> – Modern American comfort food with strong reviews and a great downtown location</li>
</ul>

<p>👉 Asheville rewards you for mixing casual spots with one or two more elevated meals.</p>

<hr>

## Check Out the Omni Grove Park Inn

<figure><figure class="media-pending"><figcaption>Original photo pending transfer.</figcaption></figure></figure>

<p>Even if you’re not staying there, <strong>Omni Grove Park Inn</strong> is worth a visit.</p>

<p>Even if you’re not staying at the <strong>Omni Grove Park Inn</strong>, it’s absolutely worth making the trip up the hill.</p>

<p>The property itself is massive and feels more like a destination than just a hotel, with some of the best mountain views in Asheville.</p>

<p>Start inside at the <strong>Great Hall</strong>, which is known for its huge stone fireplaces, high ceilings, and classic lodge feel. It’s the kind of space you can walk into and immediately understand why people come here even if they’re not overnight guests.</p>

<p>From there, head outside to the <strong>Sunset Terrace</strong>.</p>

<p>This is where the experience really stands out.</p>

<p>You’ll get:</p>

<ul>
<li>Wide-open views of the Blue Ridge Mountains</li>

<li>A relaxed atmosphere to sit and take it all in</li>

<li>One of the best sunset spots in Asheville</li>
</ul>

<p>If you have time, grab a drink or just walk the grounds — it’s an easy way to add a scenic, slower-paced moment to your trip without committing to a full activity.</p>

<p>👉 It’s a quick stop that feels like a highlight.</p>

<hr>

## Brewery Hop Around Asheville

<p>Asheville has one of the strongest brewery scenes in the country, and it’s easy to build a full afternoon or evening around it.</p>

<p>Instead of picking just one spot, it’s worth hitting a few depending on the vibe you’re looking for.</p>

### Downtown / Easy Start

<ul>
<li><strong>Wicked Weed Brewing</strong><br>One of the most well-known breweries in Asheville, especially for sours. Great central location and an easy first stop.</li>

<li><strong>Burial Beer Co.</strong><br>A more creative, slightly darker vibe with unique beers and a laid-back atmosphere.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

### River Arts District / Scenic Stops

<ul>
<li><strong>New Belgium Brewing Company</strong><br>Large outdoor space right along the river, great for a more relaxed stop with views.</li>

<li><strong>Hi-Wire Brewing</strong><br>Known for easy-drinking beers and a more casual, social feel.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

### More Local Feel

<ul>
<li><strong>Green Man Brewery</strong><br>One of Asheville’s original breweries with a more traditional vibe.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

## A Simple Brewery Route

<p>If you want to keep it easy:</p>

<p>👉 Start downtown → head toward the River Arts District → finish with a more relaxed stop</p>

<p>This lets you:</p>

<ul>
<li>Experience different styles of breweries</li>

<li>Avoid staying in one area too long</li>

<li>Build a flexible plan based on how your day is going</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<p>👉 Asheville’s brewery scene isn’t about hitting everything — it’s about finding a couple spots that match your vibe and enjoying the experience.</p>

<p><strong>Day 2</strong><br>Explore the River Arts District → head to West Asheville → stop by the Omni Grove Park Inn for views → build your own brewery route to end the day</p>

## Final Thoughts

<p>Asheville is one of those places where you don’t need a packed schedule to have a great trip.</p>

<p>Between the mountains, food, and walkable areas, it’s easy to explore at your own pace while still hitting the highlights.</p>

<p>It’s a place where a simple plan goes a long way — whether you’re taking in the views along the Blue Ridge Parkway, exploring downtown, or ending the day at a brewery or mountain overlook.</p>
