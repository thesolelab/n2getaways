<p>If you&#x27;re staying near or at the Wailea Beach Resorts in Maui, you’re surrounded by some incredible food options. Whether you’re coming back from the Road to Hana, finishing a morning at the beach, or just looking for a relaxed mid-day meal, there are several great lunch spots just minutes away.</p>

<p>From casual local favorites to healthier options and quick grab-and-go markets, if you’re <strong>staying near the Wailea Beach Resort</strong> <strong>area</strong>, here are a few spots that are actually worth your time (and money).</p>

<hr>

## Coconuts Fish Cafe

<p>If you ask locals where to get great fish tacos near Wailea, <strong>Coconuts Fish Cafe</strong> will almost always come up.</p>

<p>Their famous fish tacos are loaded with grilled fish, fresh slaw, mango salsa, and a flavorful sauce that ties everything together perfectly. At first glance, ordering two tacos might not seem like much, but the portions are generous and filling.</p>

<p>The restaurant has a relaxed island vibe that makes it an easy and enjoyable lunch stop after a morning exploring Maui.</p>

<p><strong>What to order:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Famous fish tacos</li>

<li>Coconut shrimp</li>

<li>Fish and chips</li>
</ul>

<hr>

## The Shops at Wailea

<p>The <strong>Shops at Wailea</strong> offer several dining options within a short drive of the resort. Some restaurants here lean toward the upscale side, but there are also casual spots perfect for lunch.</p>

<p>One standout option is <strong>Island Gourmet Markets</strong>, which offers a surprisingly large selection of made-to-order food, poke bowls, sandwiches, and grab-and-go items.</p>

<p>It’s a great choice if you want something quick before heading back to the beach or exploring more of South Maui.</p>

<p><strong>Why it’s worth visiting:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Quick service and lots of variety</li>

<li>Fresh poke and deli options</li>

<li>Easy stop for snacks and drinks</li>
</ul>

<hr>

## Choice Health Bar (Healthy Local Favorite)

<p>If you&#x27;re looking for something on the healthier side, <strong>Choice Health Bar in Wailea</strong> is a popular spot among both visitors and locals.</p>

<p>Their menu focuses on fresh ingredients with options like smoothie bowls, protein smoothies, wraps, and salads. It’s a great place to recharge after a morning hike or beach walk.</p>

<p>The restaurant has a laid-back atmosphere that fits perfectly with the slower pace of Maui.</p>

<p><strong>Popular choices include:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Acai bowls</li>

<li>Fresh smoothies</li>

<li>Healthy wraps and salads</li>
</ul>

<hr>

## Maui Fish'n Chips

<p>If you&#x27;re looking for something casual and consistently good, <strong>Maui Fish’n Chips</strong> is another solid lunch option just a short drive from Wailea.</p>

<p>This small local spot focuses on simple, well-prepared seafood dishes without the tourist-heavy atmosphere you’ll find in some other areas. The fish is fresh, the portions are generous, and it’s the kind of place locals stop by for a quick, satisfying meal.</p>

<p>Their fish and chips are the standout item, featuring crispy battered fish served with fries and house-made sauces. It’s a straightforward menu, but everything is done well.</p>

<p><strong>What to try:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Classic fish and chips</li>

<li>Fish tacos</li>

<li>Mochiko chicken</li>
</ul>

<p>If you want to explore something that&#x27;s not on the menu, ask for the Grilled Shrimp to be seasoned with the Volcano Fries seasoning. You can make your own sauce to go with the shrimp with using</p>

<ul>
<li>Sriracha (to taste)</li>

<li>Malt vinegar (use enough to get the desired consistency you want)</li>

<li>Soy sauce (to taste)</li>
</ul>

<p>It’s an easy stop if you&#x27;re heading between <strong>Kihei and Wailea</strong>, making it a convenient lunch option after a morning at the beach or exploring South Maui.</p>

<hr>

## Kihei Caffe (Breakfast Worth the Short Drive)

<p>While technically known as a breakfast spot, <strong>Kihei Caffe</strong> deserves a mention if you&#x27;re planning an early start to your day before heading back toward Wailea.</p>

<p>For the most authentic experience, many locals recommend going to the <strong>original location at 1945 S Kihei Rd</strong>, right across from Kalama Park.</p>

<p>The lines can get long, but they move quickly, and the food is worth the wait. It’s one of those classic Maui spots that visitors return to year after year.</p>

<p>Starting your day here can easily turn into a relaxed late breakfast that carries you through the afternoon.</p>

<hr>

## What About Restaurants at Wailea Beach Resort?

<p>The Wailea Beach Resort has several dining options on property, including <strong>Ferraro’s</strong>, which offers great ocean views and a beautiful setting for a meal.</p>

<p>However, it’s worth noting that popular resort restaurants often require <strong>reservations</strong>, and they can book up <strong>weeks in advance</strong>, especially during busy travel seasons.</p>

<p>If you’re looking for a more casual lunch without planning ahead, the nearby options listed above are excellent alternatives.</p>

<hr>

## Final Thoughts on Lunch Near Wailea Beach Resort

<p>One of the best things about staying in Wailea is the variety of food options within just a few minutes of the resort.</p>

<p>Whether you&#x27;re craving <strong>fresh fish tacos, healthy smoothie bowls, or quick grab-and-go meals</strong>, there are plenty of great lunch spots nearby.</p>

<p>Taking the time to explore some of these local favorites is one of the easiest ways to experience the food culture of Maui while enjoying the laid-back island atmosphere.</p>
