*Flying into Costa Rica and heading to La Fortuna? Here's what you should know about choosing an airport, arranging transportation, and navigating the journey to the Arenal region.*

Getting to La Fortuna, Costa Rica, takes a little planning, but it's worth the effort. Between the lush rainforest, natural hot springs, waterfalls, and views of Arenal Volcano, this part of the country offers an experience that's completely different from Costa Rica's popular beach destinations.

Before you can start exploring, though, you'll need to figure out how to get there.

One of the first decisions you'll face is whether to fly into San José or Liberia. Both airports can get you to La Fortuna, but your flight schedule, travel plans, and transportation preferences can make one more convenient than the other.

**When I visited La Fortuna, I flew into San José (SJO) and booked private transportation to La Fortuna rather than renting a car.** Having someone familiar with the roads handle the drive made the journey much easier, especially after traveling internationally.

And after seeing the road conditions firsthand, it's an approach I'd recommend considering if you're visiting Costa Rica for the first time.

Here's what to know before making your way to La Fortuna.

## Where Is La Fortuna, Costa Rica?

La Fortuna is a small town in northern Costa Rica, located near Arenal Volcano. It's one of the country's most popular destinations for travelers interested in outdoor adventures, wildlife, and relaxation.

You can spend your mornings exploring rainforest trails, afternoons visiting waterfalls, and evenings soaking in natural hot springs.

But unlike destinations where everything is within walking distance, La Fortuna's attractions and accommodations are spread throughout the surrounding area.

That makes transportation something you'll want to think about before arriving.

Most international visitors fly into one of two airports:

- **[Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO)](https://www.sjoairport.com/contacts/?lang=en):** Located in Alajuela, near San José.
- **[Guanacaste Airport (LIR)](https://www.guanacasteairport.com/en/about-us):** Located near Liberia in northwestern Costa Rica.

Neither airport is particularly close to La Fortuna, so you'll need to arrange ground transportation for the final part of your journey.

## San José vs. Liberia: Which Airport Should You Choose?

The good news is that both airports are reasonable options for visiting La Fortuna.

The challenge is figuring out which one works best for your particular trip.

### Flying into San José (SJO)

San José is where I flew into when visiting La Fortuna, and it worked well for my trip.

One thing that's helpful to know is that the airport isn't actually in downtown San José. It's located in Alajuela, which means you may not need to travel through the city center to reach La Fortuna.

From the airport, the drive generally takes around three hours, although I'd recommend allowing extra time.

As you leave the busier areas behind, the scenery gradually changes. You'll pass through smaller towns, rolling hills, and stretches of greenery as you make your way toward the Arenal region.

It's a beautiful introduction to Costa Rica, but the roads aren't always what you might expect.

During my visit, I noticed unfinished stretches of road, and rainy conditions can make driving even more unpleasant.

That's one of the reasons I was glad I'd arranged transportation instead of trying to navigate the route myself.

**San José is worth considering if:** You find a better flight schedule, plan to explore central Costa Rica, or simply prefer the flight options available through SJO.

### Flying into Liberia (LIR)

Liberia is another option, especially if you're planning to combine La Fortuna with time along Costa Rica's Pacific coast.

The drive generally passes through Cañas and Tilarán before continuing toward Lake Arenal.

This route is known for its scenery, particularly around the lake, but it also includes winding roads and sections where travel can be slower than expected.

I haven't personally taken the Liberia-to-La Fortuna route, so I can't compare the two drives from firsthand experience.

However, if your itinerary includes destinations in Guanacaste, Liberia is certainly worth considering.

**Liberia is worth considering if:** You're visiting the Guanacaste coast, find more convenient flights into LIR, or want to build an itinerary that combines beach time with the Arenal region.

### So, which airport is better?

I wouldn't make the decision based on driving distance alone.

Instead, compare your complete travel day.

Look at flight prices, arrival times, transportation costs, and where you plan to travel after La Fortuna.

A cheaper flight isn't necessarily a better deal if it means arriving late, booking an additional hotel night, or spending considerably more on transportation.

For my trip, San José was the right choice. But depending on your itinerary, Liberia might make more sense.

## Getting From San José Airport to La Fortuna

If you're flying into SJO, you'll have several ways to reach La Fortuna.

The journey typically takes around three hours under favorable conditions, but I'd avoid planning your day around an exact arrival time.

Costa Rica's roads can be unpredictable, especially during rainy weather.

Some stretches are well-maintained, while others may be unfinished, uneven, or difficult to navigate. Add winding roads and limited lighting in certain areas, and driving can become stressful if you're unfamiliar with the region.

I arranged transportation from SJO, and I'm glad I did.

Rather than worrying about directions or road conditions, I could enjoy the scenery and leave the driving to someone who knew the area.

For first-time visitors, that's a convenience worth considering.

### What about driving yourself?

Renting a car is certainly an option, but I wouldn't automatically recommend it to someone unfamiliar with driving in Costa Rica.

The roads can be challenging, particularly when it rains.

If you're comfortable navigating unfamiliar roads and want the flexibility of having your own vehicle, renting may still make sense.

Otherwise, arranging transportation can remove a lot of unnecessary stress from your arrival day.

## Getting From Liberia Airport to La Fortuna

If you're arriving through Liberia, the journey to La Fortuna generally takes around three hours, depending on conditions.

A common route travels through Cañas and Tilarán before continuing around Lake Arenal.

The scenery is one of the attractions of this drive, with hills, greenery, and views of the lake along the way.

However, the route also includes winding sections that require extra attention.

If you're driving yourself, I'd recommend making the trip during daylight and allowing plenty of time.

And just like with San José, you can arrange transportation in advance if you'd rather not drive.

## The Best Transportation Options for Getting to La Fortuna

Once you've selected your airport, you'll need to decide how you're getting to your accommodation.

There isn't one perfect option for everyone. Your budget, group size, and comfort level with driving will help determine what's best.

### 1. Private airport transfer: My preferred option

If convenience is your priority, I'd start by looking at private transportation.

I booked private transportation from SJO to La Fortuna, and it's the approach I'd seriously consider again.

With a private transfer, you arrange transportation in advance and travel directly to your accommodation.

You don't have to worry about navigating unfamiliar roads, figuring out directions, or dealing with a rental car after a long flight.

For families or groups, the cost may also be easier to justify when divided among several travelers.

The downside is that private transfers generally cost more than shared transportation.

Still, when I think about the road conditions I encountered, the convenience of having an experienced driver was worth it.

**My tip:** Before booking, confirm whether the price includes direct hotel drop-off, luggage, airport pickup, and any additional charges.

### 2. Shared shuttle: A practical middle ground

If you'd rather not drive but want something more budget-friendly than a private transfer, consider a [shared shuttle](https://ridecr.com/shuttle/).

These services transport multiple travelers along established routes, usually with scheduled departure times.

Depending on the company, you may be picked up at the airport, a nearby hotel, or another designated location.

The biggest difference compared with private transportation is flexibility.

You may need to wait for a scheduled departure or make additional stops along the way.

Still, if the schedule works with your flight, a shared shuttle can be a practical choice.

Just be sure to verify the pickup location. Not every service advertised as departing from San José necessarily picks up directly at SJO.

### 3. Rental car: Best for travelers who want flexibility

Having your own vehicle can make exploring La Fortuna easier.

Many attractions are located outside downtown, so a rental car gives you the freedom to travel without relying on tours or arranged rides.

However, I'd approach this option with some caution.

**[Driving in Costa Rica](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/costa-rica) isn't something I'd recommend taking lightly if you're unfamiliar with the roads.**

During my trip, I saw firsthand how unfinished road sections could make travel uncomfortable. Rain only adds another challenge, particularly when visibility is reduced.

That doesn't mean you shouldn't rent a car. Plenty of travelers choose to drive.

But I'd make that decision based on your experience and comfort level, not simply because renting appears cheaper.

Before reserving, check the total cost, insurance requirements, parking availability, and road conditions near your accommodation.

And don't assume you'll need a four-wheel-drive vehicle for every itinerary. The right vehicle depends on where you're staying and which roads you'll be using.

### 4. Public transportation: Best for travelers with time to spare

Public buses are another way to reach La Fortuna, although they're generally less convenient when arriving from an international airport.

Depending on your starting point, you may need to make connections and work around limited departure schedules.

If you're traveling with luggage or arriving later in the day, the extra logistics may not be worth the savings.

For travelers on a tight budget who don't mind a longer journey, public transportation is worth researching.

Just confirm current schedules and connections before making it part of your itinerary.

## Do You Need a Rental Car Once You're in La Fortuna?

This is where your accommodation and itinerary become important.

Downtown La Fortuna has restaurants, shops, and services within a relatively compact area.

However, many of the region's popular resorts and attractions are located outside town.

If you're staying at a resort and primarily planning guided excursions, you may be able to arrange transportation without needing a rental car.

Some tours include hotel pickup, while others require you to reach a designated meeting point.

On the other hand, if you want to explore independently, visit several attractions, or continue to another destination, having a vehicle can be convenient.

I'd recommend checking three things before deciding:

- Where your accommodation is located.
- Whether your planned activities include transportation.
- How often you expect to travel outside the property.

Don't rent a car simply because you think you'll need one. But don't arrive without a plan for getting around, either.

## A Few Things I'd Recommend Before Traveling to La Fortuna

### Try to arrive during daylight

This is especially important if you're planning to drive.

Winding roads, limited lighting, and changing weather can make nighttime driving more challenging than expected.

If your flight arrives late, consider arranging transportation or staying near the airport overnight.

### Give yourself extra travel time

Even if your navigation app estimates a three-hour drive, don't assume that's exactly how long the trip will take.

Traffic, rain, road conditions, and stops can all affect your arrival.

I wouldn't schedule a major activity immediately after reaching La Fortuna.

### Download offline maps

Whether you're driving or using arranged transportation, having offline maps can be helpful.

Cellular coverage may be inconsistent in some rural areas, so it's worth downloading your route before leaving the airport.

### Confirm your hotel's exact location

A property advertised as being in La Fortuna may actually be several miles outside town.

That can affect transportation costs and how easily you can reach restaurants or attractions.

### Check the weather and road conditions

Rain is part of traveling in a tropical destination, but it can also make getting around more difficult.

If you're planning to drive, check conditions before departure and ask your accommodation about any known issues along your route.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Getting to La Fortuna

### Is it better to fly into San José or Liberia for La Fortuna?

Both airports are practical options. San José may work better for trips centered around central Costa Rica, while Liberia can be convenient if you're also visiting Guanacaste. Compare flight schedules, transportation costs, and your overall itinerary before deciding.

### How long is the drive from San José to La Fortuna?

Plan for approximately 2.5 to 3.5 hours under favorable conditions, with extra time for traffic, rain, and stops.

### Can you get to La Fortuna without renting a car?

Yes. Private transfers, shared shuttles, and public transportation are available. You can also arrange transportation for activities once you arrive.

### Is driving to La Fortuna difficult?

It can be challenging for travelers unfamiliar with Costa Rica's roads. Winding routes, unfinished sections, limited lighting, and heavy rain can make driving more stressful. Arranged transportation is worth considering, particularly for first-time visitors.

### Do you need a 4x4 to visit La Fortuna?

Not necessarily. Many popular attractions and accommodations are accessible without four-wheel drive, but conditions vary. Confirm the road requirements for your specific accommodation and planned activities before renting.

## Final Thoughts: Getting to La Fortuna Without the Stress

La Fortuna is absolutely worth including on a Costa Rica itinerary, but getting there is one part of the trip I'd recommend planning ahead.

For my visit, flying into San José and booking private transportation to La Fortuna worked well. I didn't have to worry about navigating unfamiliar roads, and I could enjoy the journey instead of concentrating on every turn.

After seeing some of the road conditions, particularly the unfinished sections that can become unpleasant in the rain, I'm glad I made that decision.

That doesn't mean private transportation is the right choice for everyone. If you're comfortable driving and want the freedom to explore independently, a rental car may make sense.

But if this is your first trip to Costa Rica, don't underestimate the value of having someone familiar with the roads handle the journey.

**My biggest piece of advice? Choose the transportation option that lets you start your La Fortuna getaway feeling relaxed rather than exhausted.**

You'll have plenty of opportunities for adventure once you arrive.
