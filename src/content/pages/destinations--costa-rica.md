## Why Visit Costa Rica?

<p>Costa Rica combines tropical landscapes, outdoor adventure, and incredible natural beauty into one unforgettable destination. From lush rainforests and waterfalls to relaxing hot springs and volcano views, it offers the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation for travelers looking to experience something different.</p>
